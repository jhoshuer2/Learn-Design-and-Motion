## Learner's rationale

First, the route is the largest text at 28 points bold and the date sits below in a quieter 18 points, so the reading order is route, date, departures, then the single blue Continue button. Second, every row and the button is at least 56 points tall with 16 points between rows, and I used a 8-point spacing scale throughout. Third, states do not rely on colour: the selected row has a heavier blue outline and a check mark, and the sold-out row is dashed with the words Sold out, not available. Body text is 16 to 20 points. Contrast: dark ink on white is 17:1 and white on the blue button is 8.5:1.
