Images in this folder: poster.png (Poster, 800x1200); social-square.png (Social square, 600x600); banner.png (Web banner, 728x90)

## Learner's rationale

First, all three pieces share one system: a near-black block with cream Georgia headlines, Arial for details and a single saturated blue for the call to action, adapted to each shape. Second, the reading order is the event name, then the date and place, then Register now, in the poster, the square and the banner, with the banner using a dark ground so the name stays large at 30 points. Third, essential text is measured: cream on black is 17:1, dark ink on cream is 15:1 and white on blue is 8.5:1, all above 4.5:1 even in the small banner.
