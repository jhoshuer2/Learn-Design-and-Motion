## {objective} What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you can:

- break a layout into zones (image, headline, caption, logo) with fixed positions;
- decide which parts of a template are fixed and which may change;
- reuse one template for different content without redrawing it by eye;
- tell a templated set from a set placed freely.

## {concept} Fix the structure, change the content
A **layout template** is a structure that repeats. It decides **where** things go so that you only decide **what** goes there. For a simple post or poster the zones are usually:

- an **image zone**;
- a **headline zone**;
- a **caption or detail zone**;
- a **logo zone**, always in the same place and at the same size.

Write down what is **fixed** (zone positions, margins, logo size, type roles) and what is **flexible** (the words, the image, an accent colour from the palette). Allow a small number of variants, for example one with an image and one text-only, so the template does not break when content changes.

Templates save time, but their main value is recognition: a viewer sees the same structure again and again and starts to know the brand before reading it.

Two rules to carry through this module:

1. The logo and the zones stay put. The content moves in.
2. Plan for the longest headline, not just the one you have today.

## {realWorld} Where you meet this every day
Think of a newspaper. The masthead, headline sizes and columns are fixed, and the stories change daily. You recognise the paper before reading a word.

Now think of a shop whose flyers use a new layout each week. People have to relearn where the prices and the address are every time.

## {goodVsBad} Compare these
**Zones.** Free against fixed.

- Free: the logo appears top left on one post, bottom right on the next, and large on a third.
- Fixed: the logo is always bottom left at the same size, with the same margin.

**Change.** A longer headline.

- Weak template: the long headline overlaps the image.
- Strong template: the headline zone has room for two lines and the image zone does not move.

Why the strong versions work: the structure is decided once, with room for realistic content, so each new piece takes minutes and still matches.
