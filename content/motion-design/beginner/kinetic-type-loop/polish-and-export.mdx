## {objective} Where we are going
By the end you can:

- cut what does not help the message;
- check your animation at thumbnail size;
- export it with sensible settings.

## {concept} The idea in one minute
<<video:polish-and-export|Clutter fades away, a hold gauge shows reading time, an animation shrinks to thumbnail size, and an export checklist ticks off. Captions explain each.>>

Polish is mostly **removing**: take away what competes with your words. Give text time to be read. Shrink it to thumbnail size: if the words are still clear it will hold up on a phone. Export square at **1080 by 1080**, **30 fps**, as an **MP4 under 8 MB**.

## {demo} Play with it
Slide the size down. At what size do the words stop being readable?

<<demo:small-check|A card with the word FRESH that shrinks step by step, showing how readability changes at small sizes.>>

## {realWorld} Where you have seen this
The animations you remember are rarely the busiest. They are clear, short and finished.

## {goodVsBad} Spot the difference
- **Busy:** many shapes, effects and fonts.
- **Polished:** one idea, readable at a glance, clean loop.

Add one thing at a time and ask if it helps.

## {guided} Try it with me
1. Remove one effect or shape. Does it still work? Leave it out.
2. Make the text bigger and bolder if it is hard to read small.
3. Export 1080 by 1080, 30 fps, MP4.
4. Watch it on your phone, looping, three times.

## {check} Quick check
Answer these. You need 80% to complete the lesson. If you miss one, the explanation points you back to the part to revisit. No pressure: you can retry as often as you like.
