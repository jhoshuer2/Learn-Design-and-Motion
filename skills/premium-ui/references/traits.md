# What the six reference pages do

Each page below was studied from a screen recording. Describe, learn the principle, then make your own: copy the *reasoning*, never the artwork or wording.

## Crest (launch platform, light, isometric 3D)
- Centred serif headline "Where Builders Launch When They're *Serious.*" with the last word in a warm italic serif, tiny eyebrow above, one red pill CTA.
- Hero image is an isometric 3D object (a product being "launched") with floating UI chips, large, bleeding off the edge.
- Quiet logo strip at the bottom in grey.
- **Principle:** a single dimensional hero object that *is* the metaphor, framed by a lot of empty cream space.

## Tenure (legal AI, saturated sky blue)
- White sans headline centred on a **full-bleed illustrated sky and forest**; two small buttons.
- Below it, a large **framed app window** (document review UI) overlapping the illustration, with dense but tidy product UI.
- **Principle:** a calming illustrated world makes a complex, serious tool feel approachable; the real UI proves it is real.

## Pave (payments, warm desert)
- Serif headline with an orange italic phrase ("sleep better at night."), small avatar-stack proof line, one amber pill.
- Product dashboard (dark sidebar, warm cards, orange line chart) sits in a frame over a **desert mesa illustration** in the same orange family; logo strip below.
- **Principle:** the illustration palette and the product palette are the same family, so product and brand feel like one object.

## Ember (sign-in, pixel art)
- Split screen: the form on cream (greeting "Welcome, adventurer", provider buttons, one amber button), and a **pixel-art illustration panel** (a tree, a tent, a campfire) at the right.
- **Principle:** even the sign-in screen carries the brand world; copy has personality ("adventurer"); the form stays simple and fully usable.

## Lovable (AI builder, dark)
- Near-black ground, large left-aligned white headline with a pink-red gradient second line, small pill CTA row, quiet nav.
- **3D floating cards connected by glowing lines** show the idea (prompt to generated blocks) with depth, glow and perspective.
- **Principle:** on dark, use one glowing accent and perspective depth; keep the text area sparse so the scene can breathe.

## Plait (food app, cream)
- Serif headline "Snap your plate. *Know your day.*", italic second sentence; small star rating and a dark pill CTA.
- Below, a **bento of cards**: food photos, a phone mockup in the middle, big numerals ("10k+", "92%"), a coral card with a short promise.
- **Principle:** a row of unequal cards (photo, phone, numeral, colour block) gives rhythm and proof without long text. (Use real numbers only.)

## Shared across all six
Big serif or characterful display type; italic or colour accent on one phrase; one pill CTA; framed real UI; one hue family; generous empty space; small quiet nav; cards with soft layered shadows; consistent corner radii.
