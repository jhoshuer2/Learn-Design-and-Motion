# Premium audit (25 points)

Score each item 0 (fails), 1 (partly), 2 (clearly yes). Total /50. Under 30 reads cheap; 30 to 39 reads competent; 40+ reads premium. Fix in the order shown.

## A. Hero and first impression (fix first)
1. The first screen has **one** clear idea in a headline of 40+ px on desktop.
2. The headline has character (display face, tight leading, balanced breaks, at most one accent phrase).
3. The hero image shows the **real product** (framed window or phone), not stock.
4. One primary CTA, pill or clearly dominant; secondary is quieter.
5. A true proof line (real numbers or what it does), or none. Never invented.

## B. Type
6. At most two typefaces (display + text), loaded and bundled.
7. A defined scale (for example 12, 14, 16, 20, 28, 40, 64, 96) used consistently.
8. Line length 45 to 75 characters; body 16 px or more; line-height 1.5 to 1.65 for sans, a little more for serif.
9. Headings tighter (line-height 1.0 to 1.15, slight negative tracking); no all-caps labels above every heading.
10. Numerals are tabular where they align (tables, stats).

## C. Colour
11. One hue family plus one accent; neutrals tinted toward the hue.
12. Text contrast AA (4.5:1) everywhere, including dark mode and on images.
13. Colour is never the only carrier of meaning.
14. No more than three saturated colours on a page.

## D. Space and layout
15. A spacing scale (4 or 8 px base) used consistently.
16. Major sections separated by 96 px or more on desktop; related items grouped tightly.
17. Alignment: one main axis (centre or left) per section; edges line up.
18. A bento or card row with **unequal** sizes gives rhythm (not six identical cards).

## E. Depth and surfaces
19. Shadows are layered and soft (two or three layers, low opacity), never one heavy drop shadow.
20. Radii come from one scale; nested radii are consistent (inner = outer minus padding).
21. Borders are 1 px and low contrast; no heavy outlines.

## F. Detail and motion
22. Hover, focus and active states exist and feel considered; keyboard focus is visible.
23. One orchestrated motion moment, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
24. Empty, loading and error states are designed (and written in a human voice).
25. Images are sharp, optimised, with alt text; no layout shift.

## Fix order
Hero (1 to 5) → type (6 to 10) → space (15 to 18) → colour (11 to 14) → depth (19 to 21) → detail (22 to 25).
